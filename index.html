Rules

  • Everyone wrote their top 10 before the season. The bet settles after the last race in Abu Dhabi.
  • Error per pick is the absolute difference between predicted and actual championship position. A driver outside the top 10 counts at his real position, and a predicted driver missing from the standings counts as last.
  • The single largest error is crossed out. Score is the sum of the other nine. The mean absolute error of those nine is shown for reference.
  • Tiebreak: smaller crossed-out error wins, then the error on P1, then P2, and so on.
  • Entry 20 CHF, winner takes the whole pot.
